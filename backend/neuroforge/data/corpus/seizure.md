# Seizure risk, GABA-A and NMDA receptors

Elevated seizure risk (epileptiform activity) is reflected in increased gamma/beta EEG power and
spiking. Enhancing inhibitory GABA-A receptor tone or modulating excitatory NMDA receptor
signaling are established anti-seizure / neuroprotective mechanisms. (Illustrative summary for a
simulation; not medical advice.)
