# Neuroinflammation and TNF-alpha / COX-2

Neuroinflammation is characterized by elevated pro-inflammatory cytokines such as IL-6 and
TNF-alpha and acute-phase proteins like CRP. Microglial activation and cytokine signaling are
implicated in neurodegenerative and mood pathology. Anti-inflammatory strategies that modulate
TNF-alpha signaling or inhibit COX-2 are explored to reduce inflammatory burden. Reduced
heart-rate variability often accompanies systemic inflammation. (Illustrative summary for a
simulation; not medical advice.)
