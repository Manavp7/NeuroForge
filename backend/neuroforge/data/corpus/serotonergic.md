# Serotonergic deficit, mood, and the serotonin transporter (SERT)

Mood disorders are linked to serotonergic deficit and reduced BDNF, with frontal alpha asymmetry
observed on EEG and reduced sleep efficiency on wearables. The serotonin transporter (SERT) is a
canonical target; modulating serotonin reuptake is a well-studied mechanism. (Illustrative
summary for a simulation; not medical advice.)
