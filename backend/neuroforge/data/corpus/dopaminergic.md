# Dopaminergic deficit and the D2 receptor

Dopaminergic deficit, as seen in parkinsonian states, is associated with reduced dopamine
signaling, elevated alpha-synuclein, and exaggerated cortical beta-band oscillations on EEG.
Dopamine D2 receptor modulation and acetylcholinesterase inhibition (pro-cognitive) are common
intervention targets. Bradykinesia manifests as a reduced activity index on wearables.
