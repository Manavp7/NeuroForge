# Closed-loop adaptive therapy and uncertainty

Closed-loop systems sense patient state, propose an intervention, monitor the response, and adapt.
Uncertainty quantification is essential: noisy BCI/EEG signals and out-of-distribution molecules
should widen confidence intervals and make decisions more conservative. Doctor-in-the-loop
approval and explainability are core safety mechanisms. (Illustrative summary for a simulation;
not medical advice.)
